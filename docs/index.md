<h2 style="text-align: center;">Welcome to the HDCC Sandbox Tutorial!</h2>

The [NBDC Data Access Platform](https://nbdc-datashare.lassoinformatics.com/) includes the [HDCC Sandbox](https://nbdc-safe.lassoinformatics.com/pun/sys/dashboard). The Sandbox is a secure, cloud-based environment for researchers to access and analyze tabulated HBCD dataset. The platform provides a user-friendly interface with options for advanced and beginner users and a suite of tools such as R and Jupyter to facilitate data exploration and analysis.

### Motivation

An unprecedented volume of imaging and non-imaging phenotypic infant longitudinal data become available through large-scale open data initiatives such as the Healthy Brain Child Development ([HBCD](https://hbcdstudy.org/)) Study, spurring rapid growth of infant brain imaging studies to improve understanding of developmental processes important to mental health. While HBCD creates new opportunities to explore the brain’s interaction with the environment and integrate socioenvironmental and behavioral data with neuroimaging information, they also raise challenges in data management and analysis.
